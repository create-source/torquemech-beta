## Backwards-Compatible Migration Strategy

Future category splitting should be incremental and reversible.

Principles:

- Service codes remain stable and continue to identify the service.
- Category changes must not alter saved service identity.
- Old category keys can temporarily map to multiple new categories.
- Legacy category aliases should be supported during transition.
- Migration should be additive first, then cleanup later.
- Historical estimates, repair records, and invoice records should continue displaying the originally saved service code/name.

Recommended phases:

1. Add taxonomy metadata only. Add future category labels or tags to services while leaving current `category` ownership unchanged.
2. Add read-side compatibility. Allow old category keys to resolve to grouped new destinations without changing existing API contracts.
3. Update frontend browse/search gradually. Search can show future taxonomy labels while service codes remain unchanged.
4. Migrate content links. Repair guides, symptom pages, and hubs can update links only after legacy code resolution exists.
5. Review analytics and saved-record behavior. Confirm old links and historical records continue to resolve.
6. Only then consider live category-key changes, with tests for old and new category access.

## Compatibility Considerations By Surface

- Saved estimates: keep stored `serviceCode` and display text intact.
- Repair guides: links using `/estimator?service=...` must keep resolving.
- API consumers: preserve `/api/service/{service_code}` lookup by stable code.
- Frontend service selection: support legacy category aliases while users transition.
- Existing links/bookmarks: service-code prefill should not depend on the current category key.
- Historical repair and invoice records: do not rewrite stored records for taxonomy changes.
- Search metadata: aliases can mention future category terms before category ownership changes.

## Initial Expansion Status

Restraint & Safety Systems, ADAS & Safety Electronics, and Hybrid & EV are now live Phase 3.1 categories in `services_catalog.json`. They were introduced with additive category keys and stable service codes, without changing existing category keys or existing service assignments.

Phase 3.1 Batch 5 also makes Glass, Mirrors & Wipers, Lighting, and Interior & Accessories live categories. Related legacy services remain in Electrical, Body / Paint, ADAS & Safety Electronics, Restraint & Safety Systems, A/C & Heating, Maintenance, or Diagnostics where they already existed. Those existing services may be reassigned or dual-surfaced later only through a compatibility-safe taxonomy migration that preserves service-code lookup, saved records, and existing estimator links.

Phase 3.1 Batch 6 makes Body & Exterior a live category with additive coverage for bumper hardware, bolt-on exterior panels, doors, hood, trunk/liftgate/hatch/tailgate closures, exterior trim/accessories, and rust/corrosion inspection or referral estimates. Existing Body / Paint services, Glass/Mirrors/Wipers services, Lighting services, and Interior & Accessories services remain in their current categories to avoid duplicate service codes and saved-record churn.

Phase 3.1 Batch 7 makes Wheels, Tires & Alignment a live category with additive coverage for tire inspections, pressure and changeover work, safe tire-damage triage, vibration and ride diagnostics, TPMS, wheel hardware and fitment, spare-tire systems, alignment adjustments, and alignment prerequisite checks for ADAS. Existing Maintenance, Suspension & Steering, Drivetrain, Brakes, and ADAS services remain in their current categories to preserve service-code lookup and historical records.

Phase 3.1 Batch 8 makes Fluids, Filters & Preventive Maintenance a live category with additive coverage for preventive inspections, maintenance schedule reviews, fluid checks and top-offs, fluid condition tests, filter inspections, lubrication and protection, belt and hose condition inspections, diesel fluids, and specialty preventive fluid checks. Existing Maintenance, Cooling, Brakes, Transmission, Drivetrain, HVAC, Fuel, Hybrid & EV, and Diagnostics services remain in their current categories to avoid duplicate service codes and saved-record churn.

Phase 3.1 Batch 9 makes Specialty Diagnostics & General Inspections a live category with additive coverage for comprehensive and post-purchase inspections, general safety and reliability checks, cross-system warning and symptom diagnostics, water/wind/dust intrusion, post-impact inspections, second opinions, advanced road tests, limited teardown inspections, scan-data review, waveform testing, network diagnostics, and post-repair multi-system verification. Existing Diagnostics, Electrical, Cooling, Engine, Fuel, ADAS, Body & Exterior, Wheels/Tires/Alignment, and other system-specific diagnostic services remain in their current categories.

## Phase 3.1 Final Gap Audit

Phase 3.1 Batch 10 performed a bumper-to-bumper gap audit across all 23 live categories. No new major category was needed. High-confidence missing coverage was added to existing categories for diesel glow-plug systems, turbocharger and charge-air inspection, engine oil coolers, diesel EGR coolers, DPF regeneration and DPF sensors, air/electronic suspension, battery-drain and voltage-drop testing, CAN-bus/network and module software workflows, trailer hitch and pickup bed-cover inspection, and trailer-brake controller diagnostics.

The final audit confirmed that broad diagnostics and inspections remain separate from system-specific diagnostics. Existing diagnostic services such as `driveability_diagnosis`, `no_start_diagnosis`, `fluid_leak_diagnosis`, `parasitic_draw_test`, `overheating_diagnosis`, ADAS diagnostics, TPMS diagnostics, and system-specific repair diagnostics remain canonical in their current categories. The catalog is ready for Phase 3.1 freeze from a coverage and validation standpoint, with future work recommended only for taxonomy metadata, read-side compatibility aliases, and eventual UI surfacing rather than additional catalog expansion.

## Phase 3.2 Surfacing Validation

Phase 3.2 treats the Phase 3.1 freeze as immutable at 23 categories and 788 service codes. Validation work now focuses on catalog surfacing rather than scope expansion: category API loading, per-category service filtering, service-code lookup, searchable metadata reachability, representative Create Estimate selection metadata, Pro conversion, Repair Workspace display, completion, repair history, and final invoice rendering.

Representative validation keeps broad diagnostics in `specialty_diagnostics_inspections`, system-specific diagnostics in their owning categories, ADAS calibration in `adas_safety`, hybrid/high-voltage work in `hybrid_ev`, tire quantity services in `wheels_tires_alignment`, diesel services in their system categories, and estimate/referral body services in `body_exterior` or legacy `body_paint` as currently assigned. No category or service-code additions, removals, or renames are part of Phase 3.2.
