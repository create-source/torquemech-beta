5-Pin Relay Wiring (87a)

A 5-pin relay includes terminal 87a, which is the normally closed output. This means power can pass through 30 to 87a until the relay is energized.

Relay Pin Quick Reference

Pin Name Function
30 Battery Power Main power input.
85 Coil Ground Ground side of the relay coil.
86 Switch Signal Trigger signal from switch or control module.
87 Normally Open Receives power when the relay is energized.
87a Normally Closed Receives power when the relay is not energized.
