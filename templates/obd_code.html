Start Here (Quick Direction)

  • Check the most common failure first (coil, plug, injector, or wiring)
  • Inspect connectors and harness for damage or corrosion
  • Verify power and ground before replacing parts
  • Swap components (if applicable) to confirm fault location

Diagnostic Workflow

  1. Confirm the code and freeze frame data
  2. Inspect wiring, connectors, and obvious damage
  3. Test the most likely failed component first
  4. Verify power, ground, and signal integrity
  5. Confirm repair and clear the code

Wiring & Electrical Focus

  • Trace power and ground paths to the affected component
  • Check fuse and relay supplying the circuit
  • Look for high resistance (corrosion, loose pins)
  • Inspect harness routing near heat or moving parts
