About TorqueMech

Product overview

TorqueMech is a lightweight automotive tool that helps drivers and technicians quickly understand vehicle problems and repair costs.

The platform combines structured repair estimates with clear diagnostic guidance based on OBD-II trouble codes.

What TorqueMech Provides

  • Repair cost estimates for common services
  • Plain-language explanations of OBD-II codes
  • Quick checks and possible causes
  • Structured estimate summaries

Our Goal

TorqueMech aims to make automotive diagnostics and repair planning clearer, faster, and more accessible.

Whether you are a DIY vehicle owner or a professional technician, the goal is the same:

clear information, accurate estimates, and practical guidance.
