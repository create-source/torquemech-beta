Spark Plug Replacement Cost

Spark plug replacement cost usually depends on engine layout, plug access, and whether ignition coils, intake components, or related tune-up items add labor to the job. This page gives a clear baseline before you move into the estimator.

Estimate Spark Plug Replacement

Estimated Cost

$150 to $650+

Typical total depends on cylinder count, plug accessibility, labor rate, and whether related ignition parts are serviced at the same time.

Labor Time

1.0 to 4.0 hours

Straightforward four-cylinder jobs are usually quicker, while transverse V6 engines or buried rear-bank plugs add time.

Parts Cost

$20 to $250+

Price varies based on plug type, cylinder count, brand, and whether coils or boots are replaced during the repair.

Replacement Signs

  • Engine misfire or rough idle
  • Hesitation or reduced power under load
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Hard starting or longer crank time
  • Check engine light with misfire-related fault codes

What Affects Cost

  • Engine layout and plug access
  • Four-cylinder versus V6 or V8 labor time
  • Standard, platinum, or iridium plug choice
  • Whether intake parts or coil packs need removal
  • Condition of threads, boots, and related ignition hardware

Service Overview

Spark plug pricing can look simple, but labor changes fast once access gets tight. On many engines the real difference is whether the shop can reach the plugs directly or has to move intake parts, covers, or rear-bank components first.

  • Confirm misfire, maintenance interval, or worn-plug condition
  • Remove covers, coils, or intake parts as needed for access
  • Inspect plug wells and threads before installation
  • Install and torque the replacement plugs correctly
  • Reassemble ignition parts and verify the engine runs cleanly
