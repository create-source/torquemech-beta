Electrical Insight

{% if code_upper in ["P0100", "P0101", "P0102", "P0103", "P0104", "P0110", "P0115", "P0116", "P0117", "P0118", "P0120", "P0121", "P0122", "P0123", "P0130", "P0135", "P0140", "P0150", "P0155"] %}

This code usually points to a sensor circuit issue. The fault may be in the sensor, its wiring, connector, reference voltage, signal wire, or ground.

  • Open circuit (broken wire or unplugged connector)
  • Short to ground
  • Short to power
  • Weak or missing sensor signal
  • Corroded connector or poor ground
{% elif code_upper in ["P0560", "P0562", "P0563", "P0620", "P2500", "P2501"] %}

This code usually points to a system voltage or charging circuit issue. The problem may involve the battery, alternator, power cable, fuse link, or ground path.

  • Low system voltage
  • Voltage drop on the positive side
  • Poor engine or chassis ground
  • Weak battery or charging problem
  • Loose or corroded battery terminals
{% elif code_upper in ["P0300", "P0301", "P0302", "P0303", "P0304", "P0351", "P0352", "P0353", "P0354"] %}

This code usually points to an ignition or injector control circuit issue. The problem may involve coil control, injector pulse, power supply, or ground.

  • Weak or missing coil signal
  • Injector circuit fault
  • Poor ground connection
  • Damaged connector or wiring
  • Fault in the ignition control side of the circuit
{% else %}

This code may involve an electrical circuit issue. The problem is not always the component itself — it may also be related to wiring, connectors, power supply, or ground.

  • Open circuit
  • Short to ground
  • Short to power
  • High resistance from corrosion or loose connections
  • Faulty sensor, actuator, or control component
{% endif %}

Electrical faults should be confirmed with proper testing before replacing parts.
