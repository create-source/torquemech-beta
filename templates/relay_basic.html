Basic 4-Pin Relay Wiring

Automotive relays allow a low-current switch to control a higher current load. This diagram shows how a standard 4-pin relay is wired.

Relay Pin Quick Reference

Pin Name Function
30 Battery Power Receives constant 12V from the battery.
85 Coil Ground Ground side of the relay coil.
86 Switch Signal Trigger signal from switch or ECU.
87 Output Power sent to the load when relay activates.
