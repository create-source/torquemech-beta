Battery Replacement Cost

Battery replacement cost usually depends on battery size, cold-cranking capacity, mounting location, and whether the vehicle needs battery registration or memory-preserve steps after installation. This page gives you a mechanic-first baseline before you move into the estimator.

Estimate Battery Replacement

Average Cost

$180 to $450+

Typical total depends on battery group size, AGM versus flooded design, and whether testing or registration is part of the job.

Labor Time

0.3 to 0.8 hours

Under-hood battery swaps are quick, but trunk-mounted or buried batteries can take longer.

Parts Cost

$120 to $320+

Pricing changes with battery capacity, warranty level, brand, and whether AGM construction is required.

Common Symptoms

  • Slow cranking or no-start condition
  • Repeated jump starts
  • Low-voltage warnings or electrical resets
  • Battery failing a load or conductance test
  • Visible corrosion, leakage, or battery case swelling

What Affects Cost

  • Battery type, group size, and cold-cranking rating
  • Mounting location and access time
  • Terminal cleaning or cable-end service
  • Battery registration or system reset on newer vehicles
  • Warranty length and part quality

Service Overview

Battery quotes are best when the charging and starting system have already been checked. A good shop confirms the battery is actually weak, inspects the terminals, and makes sure an alternator or draw problem is not the real issue.

  • Test battery state of health and verify charging voltage
  • Remove covers, braces, or trunk trim as needed for access
  • Clean terminals and install the replacement battery securely
  • Register the battery or reset adaptations when required
  • Verify starting and charging performance after installation
