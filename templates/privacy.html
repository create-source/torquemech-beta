Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-03-02

1. Information We Collect

During Beta, TorqueMech collects limited information necessary to provide functionality:

  • Vehicle details (year, make, model)
  • Selected services used to generate estimates
  • Optional customer name and phone number (if entered for estimate output)
  • Feedback submissions (name/email optional, message required)

We may also automatically collect basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps for security and stability purposes.

We do not collect payment information. We do not create user accounts. We do not sell personal data.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Generate repair estimates
  • Display diagnostic guidance
  • Improve system performance and stability
  • Review and respond to feedback

3. Data Storage & Security

TorqueMech uses reasonable safeguards appropriate for a Beta application to protect stored information. However, no online system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

4. Data Retention

Draft estimates may be stored locally in your browser. Feedback submissions may be retained for internal review and product improvement.

We retain data only as long as necessary for operational purposes.

5. Third-Party Hosting

TorqueMech may be hosted on third-party infrastructure providers. These providers may process limited technical data necessary for hosting and security.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party marketing cookies.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of feedback submissions by contacting us at:

support@torquemech.com

7. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as TorqueMech evolves. Changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above.
