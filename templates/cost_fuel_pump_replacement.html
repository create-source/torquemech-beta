Fuel Pump Replacement Cost

Fuel pump replacement cost usually depends on whether the pump is mounted inside the tank, how much fuel system diagnosis is needed, and how much labor it takes to reach the module safely. This page gives a clean baseline so you can understand the job before moving into the estimator.

Open Estimator

Average Cost

$450 to $1,600+

Typical total depends on pump location, module style, labor rate, and whether the job includes a tank drop or related fuel system parts.

Labor Time

2.0 to 5.5 hours

Some vehicles offer easier service access, while others require lowering the fuel tank and handling straps, shields, and lines carefully.

Parts Cost

$150 to $900+

Price changes based on pump module design, sending unit integration, brand quality, and whether the application uses an in-tank or external pump.

Repair Difficulty

Advanced

Advanced. Many vehicles require tank access and careful handling of fuel lines and wiring.

Common Symptoms

  • Long crank or hard starting
  • Engine stalls under load or while driving
  • No-start condition with low fuel pressure
  • Whining noise from the fuel tank area
  • Loss of power during acceleration

What Affects Cost

  • In-tank versus external pump design
  • Fuel tank access and amount of fuel in the tank
  • Need for diagnosis before replacement
  • Integrated sending unit or module replacement
  • Part quality and related filter or line service
