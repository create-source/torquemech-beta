Engine Misfire

How to diagnose rough running engines, hesitation, and loss of power.

Common Symptoms

  • Rough idle or shaking engine
  • Loss of power during acceleration
  • Flashing check engine light
  • Engine hesitation or stumbling
  • Poor fuel economy

Most Common Causes

  • Worn spark plugs
  • Bad ignition coils
  • Vacuum leaks
  • Fuel injector problems
  • Low compression
  • Faulty mass airflow sensor (MAF)

Common OBD Codes

  • P0300 – Random / Multiple Cylinder Misfire
  • P0301 – Cylinder 1 Misfire
  • P0302 – Cylinder 2 Misfire
  • P0303 – Cylinder 3 Misfire
  • P0304 – Cylinder 4 Misfire

Basic Diagnostic Steps

  1. Scan the vehicle for OBD codes.
  2. Inspect spark plugs and ignition coils.
  3. Check for vacuum leaks.
  4. Test fuel injector operation.
  5. Check engine compression if misfire persists.

Typical Repair Costs

  • Spark plug replacement: $150 – $400
  • Ignition coil replacement: $200 – $600
  • Fuel injector replacement: $300 – $900
