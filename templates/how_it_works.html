What the complete workflow covers

One connected process from customer intake to repair history.

1

Add the customer and vehicle

Keep customer, vehicle, mileage, and repair information together.

2

Create the estimate

Build a clear estimate using labor, parts, services, and shop pricing.

3

Send it for approval

Give the customer a simple link to approve, decline, or decide later.

4

Start the repair

Move approved work directly into the active repair workspace.

5

Add findings when needed

Document additional labor, parts, photos, and customer decisions.

6

Complete the repair

Record completion notes and finish the final inspection.

7

Create the final invoice

Generate a professional invoice from the completed repair.

8

Keep the history connected

Return later to review the customer, vehicle, estimate, and repair history.
