A/C Compressor Replacement Cost

A/C compressor replacement cost usually depends on compressor access, refrigerant recovery and recharge, and whether the system needs extra cleanup or component replacement after a compressor failure. This page gives a clean baseline so you can understand the job before moving into the estimator.

Open Estimator

Average Cost

$900 to $2,200+

Typical total depends on compressor type, labor access, refrigerant service, and whether the repair includes a dryer, expansion valve, or flushing.

Labor Time

3.0 to 6.5 hours

Recovery, replacement, evacuation, and recharge add time, especially when the compressor is buried low in the engine bay.

Parts Cost

$350 to $1,400+

Pricing varies based on compressor design, OE versus aftermarket quality, and whether the job also requires seals, oil, or related A/C components.

Repair Difficulty

Advanced

Advanced. Refrigerant recovery, recharge, and tight access can make this a longer A/C repair.

Common Symptoms

  • Warm air from the vents with A/C selected
  • Compressor clutch not engaging or cycling abnormally
  • Grinding, whining, or rattling from the compressor area
  • Metal debris found during A/C system diagnosis
  • Repeated refrigerant pressure problems after recharge

What Affects Cost

  • Compressor location and engine bay access
  • Refrigerant recovery, evacuation, and recharge requirements
  • Need for receiver-drier, condenser, or expansion valve service
  • System contamination after internal compressor failure
  • Part type: aftermarket, premium aftermarket, or OE
