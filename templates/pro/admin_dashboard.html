Metric definitions

MRR: sum of locally stored recurring unit amount multiplied by quantity, normalized to monthly value by Stripe billing interval and interval count, for active paid or cancel-at-period-end real accounts that still have access. Canceled, expired, incomplete, unpaid, past-due, and test accounts are excluded.

Conversion: paid conversions divided by eligible completed trials. Active trials are excluded from the denominator; incomplete and past-due subscriptions are not counted as converted.
